About Yummers

What Yummers is

Yummers is a social food app for Cambodia. It's a food diary you share with friends: rate the restaurants, cafés and street food places you've eaten at, keep lists of where to go next, and see what your friends are loving.

Yummers was called HungryRaccoon until October 2026. Links to hungryraccoonapp.com still work and bring you here.

How rating works

When you've eaten somewhere, tap Liked it or Didn't like it. That's the whole rating: no stars and no score. If you want, add what you ordered, a comment and who you went with. Your ratings are private unless you choose to show them on the restaurant's page.

Finding somewhere to eat

You can browse places by neighbourhood and by cuisine, search by name, and see which places are open now. You can look around without an account; you only need one to rate places and make lists. Restaurant details come partly from public sources, including Google, so please check with a place before you go.

Where we are

Yummers is still in the making. We're testing early versions of the app, and it isn't on the App Store or Google Play yet. Follow along on Instagram and TikTok to hear when it's ready.

Questions people ask

What is Yummers?

Yummers is a free app for rating the restaurants, cafés and street food places you've eaten at in Cambodia. You make lists of where to eat, share them with friends, and see where your friends like to eat.

Is Yummers free?

Yes. Yummers is free to use, and it has no ads.

How do I get the app?

Yummers isn't on the App Store or Google Play yet. The beta is iPhone only for now and opens in November. Leave your email on the homepage and we'll send you an invite.

Where does Yummers work?

All over Cambodia: Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, Kampot and more. It doesn't cover places outside Cambodia.

Who can see my ratings and lists?

Only you, unless you choose otherwise. Your ratings and lists start private. You can show a rating on the restaurant's page, or make a list public and share its link with anyone.

Is Yummers like Beli?

It's a similar idea, made for Cambodia: you keep track of where you've eaten, make lists, and follow your friends. Instead of ranking places against each other, you just say whether you liked each one.

Can I order food or book a table?

No. Yummers helps you decide where to eat and remember what you thought. It doesn't do delivery or reservations.

A restaurant's details are wrong. How do I fix them?

Email admin@yummersapp.com with what should change and we'll fix it.
