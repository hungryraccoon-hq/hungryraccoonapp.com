Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026 (new contact addresses)

The short version

This website doesn't collect your personal information. There are no forms, no sign-ups, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. The fonts and images load from this site itself, not from third parties. The one exception is shared list pages, which fetch the list from our database provider (see below).

Who we are

HungryRaccoon is a social food app in the making for Phnom Penh, Cambodia. This policy covers the website at hungryraccoonapp.com. You can reach us at hello@hungryraccoonapp.com or on Instagram.

What this website collects

Nothing, directly. We don't ask for your name, email or location, and we don't set cookies.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, to keep its service secure and running. GitHub handles that information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't receive or use it.

When you open a link to a shared list (an address starting /l/), the page fetches that list from our database provider, Supabase, so your browser connects to Supabase too. It fetches only the list: no account, no cookies and nothing about you is sent, though Supabase, like any server, sees the IP address of the request. Supabase handles that under the Supabase Privacy Policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to our Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and TikTok pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link to them.

The HungryRaccoon app

The app isn't available to download yet. Before it launches, we'll update this policy to explain what the app collects (for example an account email, and the ratings, reviews and lists you choose to save), why, and how you can see or delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@hungryraccoonapp.com.
