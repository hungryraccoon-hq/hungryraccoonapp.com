Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

The short version

This website doesn't collect your personal information. There are no forms, no sign-ups, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. The fonts and images load from this site itself, not from third parties.

Who we are

HungryRaccoon is a social food app in the making for Phnom Penh, Cambodia. This policy covers the website at hungryraccoonapp.com. You can reach us at hungryraccoonkh@gmail.com or on Instagram.

What this website collects

Nothing, directly. We don't ask for your name, email or location, and we don't set cookies.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, to keep its service secure and running. GitHub handles that information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't receive or use it.

Links to other sites

This site links to our Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link to them.

The HungryRaccoon app

The app isn't available to download yet. Before it launches, we'll update this policy to explain what the app collects (for example an account email, and the ratings, reviews and lists you choose to save), why, and how you can see or delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hungryraccoonkh@gmail.com.
